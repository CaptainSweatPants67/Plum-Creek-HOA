# Committee management and member pages

Open **HOA administration → Committees & members** (`/admin/committees/`) with HOA administration permission and a verified authenticator. Choose an existing committee or **Add a new committee**, enter its description and responsibilities, and save. Add members with their names, roles, optional appointment dates and internal notes.

New committees and member names default to member-only visibility. Check **Show this committee on the public website** to list the committee publicly. Each name also requires its own **Show this member’s name on the public website** checkbox. An unchecked committee hides its entire roster publicly, even when individual names are checked. Active committees and current active names appear in `/member-committees/` for approved members with current rental paperwork, or authorized staff with MFA. Internal notes are only available in administration.

Uncheck **Active** to archive a committee or appointment. Archived records remain editable in administration. Start and end dates are inclusive and use the HOA's America/Chicago date. A stale save is rejected; refresh before editing again. Appointment names do not create portal accounts, verify ownership or grant staff access.

The Architectural Control Committee remains the current Board of Directors under the existing restrictions; manage those appointments in the leadership settings. Other committees have independent rosters. Existing published committee descriptions and names were carried forward from the old public page; the already-resigned appointment remains archived. No homeowner or historical-contact records were imported or verified.

The public `/committees/` page and volunteer choices now load the public directory through a narrow database function. No fixed named roster is exported into public HTML or used on failure. Visibility changes apply on the next directory load or window focus. The member directory, member library and My information pages share the public site's green and cream colors, serif headings and entrance photo, while preserving private navigation and the application → pending → approved workflow. Admin pages retain their compact blue interface.
